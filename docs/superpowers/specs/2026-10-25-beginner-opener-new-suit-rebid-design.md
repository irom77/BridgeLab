# Beginner Opener New-Suit Rebid Design

After an uncontested one-level new-suit response, opener can show a separate four-card suit with a legal one-level rebid.

## Behavior

- Apply only when this bot opened one of a suit and partner made an uncontested one-level response in a different suit.
- With four or more cards in a higher-ranking suit that can be bid at the one level, rebid that suit.
- Prefer the four-card major raise already defined when opener has four-card support for responder's major.
- Keep existing balanced notrump and six-card opening-suit rebids as fallbacks.
- Use the same behavior under the current SAYC and 2/1 beginner labels.

## Scope

This covers only natural one-level second-suit rebids. Two-level second suits, reverses, competitive auctions, and complete opener rebid structures remain out of scope.
