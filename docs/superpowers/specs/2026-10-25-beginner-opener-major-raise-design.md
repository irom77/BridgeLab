# Beginner Opener Major Raise Design

After partner makes an uncontested one-level new-suit response, opener may raise responder's major when holding four-card support and a minimum opening hand.

## Behavior

- Recognize only the opener's turn after partner's one-level suit response and only when the auction is uncontested.
- With 12–15 HCP and four or more cards in responder's major, bid 2 of responder's major.
- Prefer this raise over balanced notrump rebids because it establishes the known major fit.
- Preserve the existing balanced rebids and six-card opening-suit rebid for hands without this fit.
- Apply the same behavior to the current SAYC and 2/1 beginner labels; this remains a partial agreement.

## Scope

Keep the implementation in `packages/bots`. Do not add competitive raises, jump raises, or a general opener rebid tree.
