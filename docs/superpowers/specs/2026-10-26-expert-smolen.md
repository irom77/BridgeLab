# Expert Smolen

After an uncontested 1NT opening, Stayman 2♣, and opener's 2♦ denial, Expert responder with 10+ HCP and a 5-4 major pattern jumps to three of the four-card major, showing five or more cards in the other major and forcing to game. Opener raises the longer major directly to game with three-card support; otherwise opener bids 3NT. Responder passes 3NT with a five-card longer major, or corrects to 4M with six or more cards. The convention is Expert-only; lower skill levels retain natural responses.
