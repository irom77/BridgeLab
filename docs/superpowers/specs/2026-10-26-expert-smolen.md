# Expert Smolen

After an uncontested 1NT opening, Stayman 2♣, and opener's 2♦ denial, Expert responder with 10+ HCP and a 5-4 major pattern jumps to three of the four-card major, showing five or more cards in the other major and forcing to game. Opener raises the shown four-card major directly to game with three-card support; otherwise opener bids 3NT. After 3NT, responder may place 4M in the five-card major. The convention is Expert-only; lower skill levels retain natural responses.
