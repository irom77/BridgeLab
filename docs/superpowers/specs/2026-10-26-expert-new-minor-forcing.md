# Expert New Minor Forcing

Add an Expert-only New Minor Forcing convention after an uncontested minor opening, one-level major response, and opener's 1NT rebid.

Responder's bid of the unbid minor asks about opener's major holdings and promises at least invitational values (10+ HCP in this bot). Opener first raises responder's major with three-card support, then shows a four-card other major, and otherwise denies both by bidding 2NT. Responder places game in a major with a fit, or bids 3NT with balanced game values and stoppers; with invitational values responder may invite in a major only with an eight-card fit or in notrump with a balanced hand and stoppers, and may sign off in a playable suit.

The convention applies only to Expert, uncontested auctions. Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, SAYC, and competitive continuations retain their current behavior. The convention is inspired by ACBL's New Minor Forcing description; its thresholds and limited continuation rules are explicit product choices for this bot.
