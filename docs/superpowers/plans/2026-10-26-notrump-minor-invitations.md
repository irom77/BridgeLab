# Invitational minor responses to 1NT

## Scope

After a 1NT opening, responder with 10–15 HCP, a six-card minor, and two of the top three honors bids 3♣ or 3♦ to invite 3NT. Opener accepts with a top honor and at least two cards in that minor; otherwise opener passes.

## Approaches

- Use 3♣/3♦ as natural signoffs, which leaves no invitation for a long, strong minor.
- Use 3♣/3♦ as game invitations to 3NT, retaining the existing natural weak-minor signoff relay at 2♠.

Use the invitational treatment described by the ACBL [SAYC reference](https://www.acblunit390.org/Simon/sayc.htm#1nt).

## Verification

- Cover both minor invitations in SAYC and 2/1, opener acceptance with a top honor, and opener's pass without one.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
