# 1NT minor-suit signoff

## Scope

In SAYC and 2/1, responder with fewer than 8 HCP, no four-card major, and a six-card minor bids artificial 2♠. Opener relays to 3♣; responder passes with clubs or corrects to 3♦ with diamonds.

## Approaches

- Keep 2♠ natural and leave weak minor hands to pass; simple but misses a common partscore contract.
- Use 2♠ as the minor relay; this adds one short auction and preserves Stayman and major transfers.

Use the minor relay, consistent with the ACBL [Standard Yellow Card](https://web2.acbl.org/documentLibrary/play/sayc_card.pdf), which lists the 2♠ minor signoff relay.

## Verification

- Cover both minor selections, opener's forced 3♣ relay, and responder's pass or diamond correction in SAYC and 2/1.
