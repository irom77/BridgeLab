# Balanced 20–21 HCP rebid after a major opening

## Scope

After an uncontested 1M–1NT response in SAYC or 2/1, opener rebids 3NT with a balanced 20–21 HCP hand. The existing 18–19 HCP 2NT rebid remains unchanged.

## Verification

- Cover both bidding styles with a balanced 20 HCP opener.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
