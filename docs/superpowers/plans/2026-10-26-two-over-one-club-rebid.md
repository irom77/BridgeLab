# 2/1 natural club rebid after 1M–2♦

## Scope

In an uncontested, unpassed 2/1 game force after 1M–2♦, opener with 12–17 HCP can show four clubs naturally with 3♣ when no higher-priority rebid applies. Responder shows a four-card side major, places 5♣ with four-card support, rebids 3♦ with six diamonds, or bids 3NT without a fit or long suit. A three-card fit for opener's major takes priority.

## Verification

- Test the opener's natural 3♣ rebid.
- Test responder placement with a four-card side major, four-card club support, and no fit/long suit.
- Test the six-diamond rebid, opener's raise, and responder's final 5♦ game placement.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
