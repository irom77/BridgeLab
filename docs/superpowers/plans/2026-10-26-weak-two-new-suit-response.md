# Weak-two natural new-suit response

## Scope

After partner opens a weak 2♦/2♥/2♠, an Intermediate responder with a five-card new suit and 10–14 HCP bids that suit at the cheapest legal level. The bid forces one round. Opener raises a new major with three-card support, repeats the weak suit with 5–8 HCP, or with 9–11 HCP shows an outside feature or bids 3NT. After opener's minimum, responder places major game with 13+ HCP; otherwise the auction may stop.

## Approaches

- Treat all new suits as non-forcing and leave responder without a way to invite game naturally.
- Use the SAYC forcing new-suit response and distinguish opener's minimum from maximum in the rebid.

Use the one-round force and strength-based rebids, following the ACBL [SAYC weak-two response description](https://www.acblunit390.org/Simon/sayc.htm#weak-twos).

## Verification

- Cover a natural five-card response, opener's minimum rebid, maximum feature rebid, and responder's game placement.
