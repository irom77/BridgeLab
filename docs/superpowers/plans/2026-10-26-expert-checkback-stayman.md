# Expert Checkback Stayman Inquiry and Replies

## Scope

After an uncontested 1♣/1♦–1♥/1♠–1NT auction, Expert responder with 10+ HCP and a five-card response major or four-card other major uses artificial 2♣ Checkback Stayman. Opener shows three-card support for responder's major, a four-card other major, or denies both with 2♦. This slice adds the inquiry and opener's shape-showing replies; the 2♣ call retains its natural meaning below checkback strength.

## Implementation

Add the artificial inquiry and opener's shape-showing replies to Expert bidding only. SAYC and 2/1 beginner/intermediate bidding retain their current meanings.

## Verification

- Cover responder's inquiry at invitational strength and its natural fallback below that range.
- Cover opener's three-card fit, four-card side major, and denial responses.
- Run the bot test, root test, typecheck, lint, and whitespace checks.

The reply structure follows the simple Checkback Stayman treatment described by [ACBL Unit 390](https://www.acblunit390.org/Simon/checkback-stayman.htm).
