# SAYC 1♦–2♣ natural diamond rebid

## Scope

Complete the natural opener continuation for an uncontested SAYC 1♦–2♣ auction when opener has five or more diamonds and 12–18 HCP. Opener rebids 2♦. Responder raises to 3♦ with four-card support and 10+ HCP.

## Evidence

The ACBL SAYC booklet describes 2♣ after a 1♦ opening as a natural forcing response and says opener's rebids are natural: https://web2.acbl.org/documentLibrary/play/sayc_book.pdf

## Verification

- Focused bot tests cover opener's natural rebid and responder's supported raise.
- Run the workspace `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, and `pnpm lint` scripts.
