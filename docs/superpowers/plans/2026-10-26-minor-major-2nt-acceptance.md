# Responder continuation after a minor opening and 2NT rebid

## Scope

In an uncontested auction after 1m–1M–2NT, responder with a balanced hand and 8+ HCP bids 3NT; with fewer than 8 HCP, responder passes. Apply the same continuation in SAYC and 2/1.

## Verification

- Check both configured styles at 7 and 8 HCP.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
