# Expert Blackwood after a splinter raise

## Scope

After an Expert responder makes a splinter raise and opener accepts the major fit by bidding game, responder with 18+ HCP can ask for aces with 4NT. Opener answers using the existing 5♣–5♠ Blackwood responses. Responder bids six of the agreed major when at least three aces are known; otherwise responder signs off in five of the major when legal, or passes the five-level response.

## Verification

- Cover the ace ask, opener's ace-count response, and slam placement when three aces are known.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
