# Natural lower side-suit opener rebids

## Scope

After an uncontested one-level suit response, an unbalanced opener with 12–18 HCP can show a separate four-card suit that ranks below the opening suit at the two level. Balanced notrump ranges, a major fit raise, and existing higher-suit rebids retain priority. Applies to SAYC and 2/1 auctions.

## Verification

- Cover an uncontested 1♥–1♠–2♦ auction in both styles.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
