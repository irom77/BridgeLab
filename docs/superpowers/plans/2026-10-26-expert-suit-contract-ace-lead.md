# Expert ace leads against suit contracts

## Scope

Expert keeps Advanced's selected opening-lead suit but leads its ace when that lead would otherwise underlead the ace against a suit contract. Notrump opening leads and leads from other suits remain unchanged.

## Evidence

The [ACBL opening-lead lesson](https://web2.acbl.org/documentLibrary/education/intro_to_bridge_online/Online_Class_11/C11_Online_Student_Manual.pdf) advises against underleading an ace against a suit contract.

## Verification

- Test that Expert leads the ace from the selected suit against a suit contract.
- Test that Expert retains fourth-highest from that suit against notrump.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
