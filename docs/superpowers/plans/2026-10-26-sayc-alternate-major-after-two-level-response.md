# SAYC alternate major after a two-level response

## Scope

After an uncontested SAYC 1M–2m auction, when opener rebids a natural side suit, responder with 10+ HCP and four cards in the unbid major shows it naturally at the two level. Opener bids 3M with 12–15 HCP or 4M with 16+ HCP when holding at least three-card support; without the fit, opener continues in the original major at the three level.

## Verification

- Cover the responder's alternate-major bid, opener's supported raise, and opener's no-fit continuation.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
