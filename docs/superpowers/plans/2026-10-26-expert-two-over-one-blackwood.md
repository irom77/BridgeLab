# Expert Blackwood after a 2/1 major fit

## Scope

After an unpassed direct 2/1 game-forcing response establishes a major fit, Expert responder with 18+ HCP can ask for aces with 4NT directly over opener’s three-level raise that establishes the major fit. This includes a fit found after opener rebids 2♦ and responder shows a four-card side major at the two level. It also includes a natural side-major fit: opener rebids the alternate major after a direct minor response, responder raises that major to game, and opener can continue Blackwood. A passed responder’s later two-level response is not treated as game-forcing. Opener answers using 5♣–5♠; responder bids six of the agreed major when at least three aces are known, otherwise signs off in five when legal or passes. Once all four aces are known, responder can ask for kings with 5NT; opener answers at the six level, and responder bids a grand slam only when all four kings are confirmed.

## Verification

- Cover the ace ask, opener answer, and slam placement in 1♠–2♥–3♥–4NT.
- Cover the side-major route 1♠–2♣–2♦–2♥–3♥–4NT through the 5NT king ask and final slam placement.
- Cover the natural side-major route 1♥–2♦–2♠–4♠–4NT through the 5NT king ask and final slam placement.
- Verify that a passed responder does not enter direct minor-fit, 2♣–2♦ side-major, or natural side-major Blackwood routes.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
