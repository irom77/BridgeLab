# Expert Blackwood after a 2/1 major fit

## Scope

After a direct 2/1 game-forcing response establishes a major fit, Expert responder with 18+ HCP can ask for aces with 4NT directly over opener’s three-level raise that establishes the major fit. Opener answers using 5♣–5♠; responder bids six of the agreed major when at least three aces are known, otherwise signs off in five when legal or passes.

## Verification

- Cover the ace ask, opener answer, and slam placement in 1♠–2♥–3♥–4NT.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
