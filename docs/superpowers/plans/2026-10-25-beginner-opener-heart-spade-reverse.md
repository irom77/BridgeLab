# Higher side-suit reverse after 1♥–1NT

## Goal

Extend the limited beginner opener continuations after a major opening and a 1NT response.

## Approach

After 1♥–1NT, with at least five hearts, opener can rebid 2♠ with a four-card spade suit and 16+ HCP. Prefer an available four-card lower side suit first. This preserves the extra-strength requirement of a reverse.

## Verification

Add tests for the 16+ HCP reverse and for passing with a weaker hand that has the same shape.
