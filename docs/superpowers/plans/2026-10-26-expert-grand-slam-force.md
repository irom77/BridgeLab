# Expert Grand Slam Force after Jacoby 2NT

## Scope

In Expert auctions, after an uncontested 1M–2NT Jacoby raise and opener's direct 4M rebid without showing shortness, responder asks with 5NT when holding at least 18 HCP and two of the top three trump honors. Opener bids 7M with two or more of A/K/Q in trumps and 6M otherwise.

## Verification

- Cover the responder's ask and both opener honor-count answers.
- Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `git diff --check`.
